# Ailtir Go/No-Go Framework — UK

This framework is calibrated for UK main contractors bidding public works under the Procurement Act 2023 and private work under JCT / NEC4.

## Mandatory Gates (Pass/Fail)
If any of these fail, the bid is an automatic NO-GO unless signed off by a Director.

1. **Accreditations:** Do we hold the SSIP membership required by the notice (CHAS, SafeContractor, Constructionline Gold, or equivalent), plus the ISO certifications required (typically 9001 for quality, 14001 for environmental, 45001 for occupational health & safety)?
2. **Turnover Threshold:** Is our average turnover over the last 3 years at least 1.5x to 2x the annualised contract value? (Common threshold on Procurement Act 2023 selection criteria and Constructionline Gold.)
3. **Bonding / Insurance:** Can we secure the required Performance Bond (typically 10% of contract value), Parent Company Guarantee if required, and the specified insurance limits (Employer's Liability, Public Liability, and PI if contractor design is involved)?
4. **Capacity:** Do we have a site management team available for the proposed start date?
5. **Building Safety Act 2022 (HRB scope only):** If the project is a Higher-Risk Building (18m+ residential or 7+ storeys with ≥2 dwellings), do we have Principal Contractor competency evidence under BSA 2022, and can we resource the "golden thread" of information duties?
6. **Modern Slavery Act 2015:** If our turnover is ≥£36m, do we have a current published s.54 Modern Slavery Statement on our website?
7. **Carbon Reduction Plan:** For central government contracts >£5m/year, do we have a compliant CRP under PPN 06/20?

## Weighted Scoring Matrix (Out of 100)

### 1. Client & Relationship (30 points)
- Existing client with good payment history and prior Contract Details Notice referencing us favourably (30)
- Known client, neutral history (20)
- New client, good reputation and clean Pipeline Notice history (15)
- New client, unknown or poor reputation (0)

### 2. Sector & Experience (25 points)
- Core sector, we have 3+ identical case studies (25)
- Adjacent sector, we have similar case studies (15)
- New sector, weak references (5)

### 3. Commercial & Contract (25 points)
- Standard unamended JCT SBC/DB or NEC4 ECC Option A, fair risk allocation (25)
- NEC4 Option C target-cost with balanced pain/gain, or JCT with light amendments (20)
- Heavily amended JCT (extensive Z clauses / Schedule of Amendments) or NEC4 with hostile Z-clauses — negotiable (15)
- Onerous amendments, fixed-price D&B with high design risk, or fitness-for-purpose beyond PI cover (5)

### 4. Competition & Procurement (20 points)
- Negotiated or 2-stage procurement, or Competitive Flexible Procedure with strong pre-market engagement (20)
- Restricted / Competitive Tender with pre-qualified list of 4–6 (15)
- Framework Call-Off where we are on the panel (15)
- Open Procedure (10+ bidders, price-driven) (5)

## Scoring Thresholds
- **80–100:** Strong GO. Commit full bid resources.
- **60–79:** Marginal GO. Needs Director review of weak areas.
- **<60:** NO-GO. Decline politely; consider a clarification-only response if the authority is strategic.
