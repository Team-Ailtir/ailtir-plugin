# Ailtir Go/No-Go Framework

This framework is calibrated for Irish main contractors bidding CWMF and private sector work.

## Mandatory Gates (Pass/Fail)
If any of these fail, the bid is an automatic NO-GO unless signed off by a Director.
1. **Accreditations:** Do we hold the required Safe-T-Cert grade and CIRI registration?
2. **Turnover Threshold:** Is our average turnover over the last 3 years at least 1.5x to 2x the annualized contract value? (Standard CWMF requirement).
3. **Bonding/Insurance:** Can we secure the required performance bond (typically 10-12.5%) and PI insurance?
4. **Capacity:** Do we have a site management team available for the proposed start date?

## Weighted Scoring Matrix (Out of 100)

### 1. Client & Relationship (30 points)
- Existing client with good payment history (30)
- Known client, neutral history (20)
- New client, good reputation (15)
- New client, unknown/poor reputation (0)

### 2. Sector & Experience (25 points)
- Core sector, we have 3+ identical case studies (25)
- Adjacent sector, we have similar case studies (15)
- New sector, weak references (5)

### 3. Commercial & Contract (25 points)
- Standard RIAI/PW-CF, fair risk allocation (25)
- Heavily amended, but negotiable (15)
- Onerous amendments, fixed price D&B with high design risk (5)

### 4. Competition & Procurement (20 points)
- Negotiated or 2-stage (20)
- Restricted tender (pre-qualified list of 4-6) (15)
- Open tender (10+ bidders, price-driven) (5)

## Scoring Thresholds
- **80-100:** Strong GO. Commit full bid resources.
- **60-79:** Marginal GO. Needs Director review of weak areas.
- **<60:** NO-GO. Decline politely.
