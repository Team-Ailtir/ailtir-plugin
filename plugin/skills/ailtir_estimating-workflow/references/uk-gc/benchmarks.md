# Estimating Benchmarks — UK Construction

*See `rate-library/references/uk-gc/rates-2026.md` for full rate data (BCIS median rates, CIJC labour, regional indices).*

## Standard Gap Checklist (UK JCT / NEC4 Context)

### Preliminaries (NRM1)
- [ ] Site establishment (welfare cabins to CDM 2015 Schedule 2 standards, hoarding, temporary roads)
- [ ] Site management (Contract Manager, Site Manager, Engineer, Quantity Surveyor)
- [ ] CDM 2015 duty-holder resourcing (Principal Designer / Principal Contractor)
- [ ] Site Safety Officer, first aiders, mental-health first aiders
- [ ] Temporary services (power, water, telecoms)
- [ ] Site security and CCTV
- [ ] Traffic management plan (especially for urban sites and TfL/local-highway approvals)
- [ ] Site disestablishment and make-good

### Contractual & Insurance (JCT / NEC4 Specific)
- [ ] Performance Bond (typically 10% of contract value, cost ~0.5–1.5% of bond)
- [ ] Parent Company Guarantee (if the Contractor is a subsidiary; some employers accept in lieu of a bond)
- [ ] Collateral Warranties or third-party rights (JCT SBC Section 7 / NEC4 X8 or X11)
- [ ] Retention Bond (in lieu of cash retention — where offered)
- [ ] Contract Works (CAR) Insurance
- [ ] Employer's Liability Insurance (statutory minimum £5m; most contracts require £10m+)
- [ ] Public Liability Insurance (typically £10m minimum on major works)
- [ ] Professional Indemnity Insurance (only where D&B or contractor design portion applies — typically £5m–£25m depending on project)

### Compliance & Regulatory
- [ ] SSIP accreditation renewal costs (CHAS, SafeContractor, Constructionline)
- [ ] Building Safety Act 2022 compliance (Gateway 2 and 3 for Higher-Risk Buildings — 18m+ residential or 7+ storeys with ≥2 dwellings)
- [ ] "Golden thread" information-management overhead (HRB only)
- [ ] Carbon Reduction Plan production and reporting (PPN 06/20; contracts >£5m/year with central government)
- [ ] Modern Slavery Act s.54 statement production (turnover ≥£36m)
- [ ] Social Value plan production (PPN 06/21; central government contracts)

### Logistics
- [ ] Tower crane / mobile crane hire and heavy lifts (check TfL/local crane oversail licences in London)
- [ ] Specialist equipment hire
- [ ] Waste management (Site Waste Management Plan) and skip hire — check landfill tax where applicable
- [ ] Material testing (concrete cube crushing, UKAS-accredited lab fees)
- [ ] Community Infrastructure Levy (CIL) and s.106 obligations — usually Employer cost but verify contractual position

### Post-Completion
- [ ] Rectification Period (formerly Defects Liability Period) — typically 12 months, extended to 24 on some public works
- [ ] O&M manuals and as-built information (HRB projects require the full golden thread of information)
- [ ] Commissioning, soft-landings, and handover
- [ ] BSA "handover of information" duties on HRB projects (Regulation 45 of the Building Regulations Etc. (Amendment) (England) Regulations 2023)

## Reconciliation Benchmarks

| Check | Acceptable Range | Action if Outside Range |
|---|---|---|
| Prelims as % of Directs | 10–14% (standard); 14–20% (complex) | Review staffing and site establishment |
| Contingency | 3–8% | Increase if ground conditions or design is uncertain |
| Margin | 3–8% (competitive), 8–12% (negotiated) | Discuss with Director before submitting |
| Performance Bond cost | 0.5–1.5% of bond value | Verify with bonding provider |
| BSA HRB compliance overhead | 0.5–1.5% of contract value | Verify with the Principal Designer and information manager |
