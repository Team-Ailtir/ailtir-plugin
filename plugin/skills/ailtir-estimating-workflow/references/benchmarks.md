# Estimating Benchmarks — Irish Construction

*See `ailtir-rate-library/references/irish-rates-2025.md` for full rate data.*

## Standard Gap Checklist (Irish PW-CF Context)

### Preliminaries
- [ ] Site establishment (welfare cabins, hoarding, temporary roads)
- [ ] Site management (Project Manager, Site Manager, Engineer, QS)
- [ ] Safety Officer and Safe-T-Cert compliance costs
- [ ] Temporary services (power, water, telecoms)
- [ ] Site security and CCTV
- [ ] Traffic management (especially for urban sites)
- [ ] Site disestablishment and make-good

### Contractual & Insurance (PW-CF Specific)
- [ ] Performance Bond (typically 10% of contract value, cost ~1-2% of bond)
- [ ] CAR (Contractor's All Risk) Insurance
- [ ] Employer's Liability Insurance
- [ ] Public Liability Insurance
- [ ] BCAR compliance costs (Assigned Certifier, Design Certifier)

### Compliance
- [ ] CIRI registration costs (if not already registered)
- [ ] Safe-T-Cert annual audit cost
- [ ] Construction Stage Safety Plan preparation
- [ ] PSDP/PSCS coordination costs

### Logistics
- [ ] Cranage and heavy lifts
- [ ] Specialist equipment hire
- [ ] Waste management and skip hire
- [ ] Material testing (concrete cubes, soil tests)

### Post-Completion
- [ ] Defects Liability Period (DLP) costs — typically 12 months
- [ ] O&M manuals and as-built drawings
- [ ] Commissioning and handover

## Reconciliation Benchmarks

| Check | Acceptable Range | Action if Outside Range |
|---|---|---|
| Prelims as % of Directs | 8–15% | Review staffing and site establishment |
| Contingency | 3–8% | Increase if ground conditions or design is uncertain |
| Margin | 4–12% | Discuss with Director before submitting |
| Performance Bond cost | 1–2% of bond value | Verify with bonding company |
