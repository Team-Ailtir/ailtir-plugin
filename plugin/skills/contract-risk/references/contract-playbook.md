# Ailtir Contract Playbook

This playbook defines standard acceptable commercial positions for Irish contractors.

## 1. PW-CF (Public Works Contract Forms)
These are mandatory for Irish public sector works. They are notoriously inflexible and place significant risk on the contractor.

**Standard Positions:**
- **Form:** PW-CF1 (Employer Designed, >€5m) or PW-CF2 (Contractor Designed, >€5m).
- **Time Bars:** 20 working days to notify a delay or claim. This is a strict condition precedent. Failure to notify = loss of entitlement.
- **Retention:** Typically 3% to 5%, with half released at Substantial Completion.
- **Liquidated Damages:** Must be a genuine pre-estimate of loss.
- **Weather:** Only "exceptionally adverse weather" grants an extension of time (EOT).

**What to Flag (Red Risks):**
- Any attempt to amend the standard PW-CF clauses (Schedule Part 1K).
- Unrealistic programme durations given the scope.
- Heavy reliance on Provisional Sums.

## 2. RIAI 2025
The standard form for private commercial building in Ireland.

**Standard Positions:**
- **Time Bars:** Clause 30 requires notice of delay within 10 working days of the event.
- **Retention:** Typically 5%, half released at Practical Completion.
- **Payment:** Certificates issued within 10 working days, payment within 14 working days of certificate.

**What to Flag (Red Risks):**
- Amendments deleting the contractor's right to suspend for non-payment.
- Amendments extending the payment period beyond 30 days.
- Amendments making the contractor responsible for design errors in the Employer's design (if not a D&B contract).
