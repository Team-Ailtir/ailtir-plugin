# UK Construction Rates Reference 2026

*Sources: BCIS All-in Tender Price Index (Q2 2026 update), Construction Industry Joint Council (CIJC) Working Rule Agreement rates effective from June 2026, BCIS median £/m² by building type.*

**Note on data currency:** Cost rates move quarterly. This file captures the mid-2026 baseline; any figure quoted downstream must be cross-referenced against the current live BCIS data by the user's estimator before submission.

## Statutory / Nationally Agreed Labour Rates (CIJC Working Rule Agreement)

The CIJC WRA is the most widely referenced hourly-rate framework for the UK construction industry. It is not statutory (unlike the Republic of Ireland's SEO), but is adopted by most main contractors as the labour-rate benchmark and is often referenced in bid clarifications.

| CIJC Grade | Basic Hourly Rate (2026) |
|---|---|
| Craft Rate (skilled tradesperson — bricklayer, carpenter, formworker, plasterer, painter) | £18.87 |
| Skilled Operative Grade 1 (advanced scaffolder, banksman, driver-operator, steel fixer) | £18.34 |
| Skilled Operative Grade 2 | £17.32 |
| Skilled Operative Grade 3 | £16.63 |
| Skilled Operative Grade 4 (semi-skilled) | £15.94 |
| General Operative | £14.19 |

*Note: London and specific project agreements often apply "plus rates" and site allowances that lift the effective hourly cost by 15–35% above CIJC minimums. Verify against the specific site's negotiated rate before use.*

**Employer Pension Contribution (auto-enrolment minimum):** 3% of qualifying earnings.
**Employer NIC:** 15% above the secondary threshold (as of 2026).

**Labour Burden Uplift for Estimating:** Add approximately **50–55%** to the CIJC basic rate to arrive at an all-in labour rate. This covers NIC (15%), pension (3–5%), holiday and sick pay (~12%), CITB levy, PPE, medicals, training, and non-productive time. Regional adjustment applies — London projects can push to 60%+ before overhead.

*Example: Craft rate all-in = £18.87 × 1.52 ≈ £28.68/hr (national average; add London plus rates on top).*

## Overtime Rates (CIJC WRA — standard model)

- Monday–Friday after normal hours (first 4): Time and a quarter (1.25×)
- Monday–Friday after 4 hours' overtime: Time and a half (1.5×)
- Saturday first 4 hours: Time and a half
- Saturday after 4 hours: Double time
- Sunday: Double time
- Public Holiday: Double time + day in lieu
