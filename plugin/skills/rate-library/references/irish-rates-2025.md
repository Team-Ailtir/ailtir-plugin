# Irish Construction Rates Reference 2025/2026

*Sources: WRC Sectoral Employment Order (Construction Sector) 2024, Buildcost Cost Guide H1 2025, SCSI Tender Price Index Feb 2026*

---

## Statutory Labour Rates (SEO — Effective 01 August 2025)

| Category | Basic Hourly Rate (Aug 2025) | Basic Hourly Rate (Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Craftsperson (Bricklayer, Carpenter, Electrician, Plumber, Painter, Plasterer) | €23.00 | €23.74 |
| Category A Worker (Advanced Scaffolder, Crane Driver, Steel Fixer) | €22.32 | €23.03 |
| Category B Worker (Skilled Operative > 2 yrs) | €20.71 | €21.37 |
| New Entrant Operative | €16.74 | €17.28 |

**Employer Pension Contribution (SEO Mandatory):** €31.87/week (€6.37/day)
**Employer Sick Pay Contribution:** €2.37/week

**Labour Burden Uplift for Estimating:** Add approximately **40%** to the basic rate to arrive at an all-in labour rate for estimating purposes. This covers PRSI (employer's share ~11%), pension, sick pay, holiday pay, and non-productive time.

*Example: Craftsperson all-in rate = €23.00 × 1.40 = ~€32.20/hr*

---

## Overtime Rates
- Monday–Friday after normal hours: Time + half
- Saturday first 4 hours: Time + half
- Saturday after 4 hours: Double time
- Sunday: Double time
- Public Holiday: Double time + additional day's leave
