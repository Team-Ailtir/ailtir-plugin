# Ailtir Win Themes (Irish Market)

Use these core themes when drafting quality responses for Irish tenders.

## 1. Local Supply Chain & Delivery Certainty
- **Theme:** We use established, local subcontractors.
- **Proof Point:** Reduces risk of delays, ensures rapid mobilization, supports the local economy.

## 2. Robust Quality & Safety Management
- **Theme:** We hold Safe-T-Cert and CIRI registration.
- **Proof Point:** Demonstrates compliance with Irish building regulations (BCAR) and a proven track record of zero-incident sites.

## 3. Collaborative Problem Solving
- **Theme:** We operate a "no-surprises" culture.
- **Proof Point:** Early engagement on design coordination, proactive RFI management, and transparent commercial reporting.

## 4. Sustainable Construction
- **Theme:** We manage waste and energy efficiently.
- **Proof Point:** High recycling rates, compliance with green public procurement guidelines, and experience with LEED/BREEAM/NZEB standards.
