# Contractual Notice Templates — UK General Contractor

Use these structures when drafting formal notices under UK standard forms. Every template below assumes the notice is a bona fide, fact-based communication — never draft anything that admits liability.

## 1. NEC4 ECC — Early Warning Notice (Clause 15)

**Reference:** Clause 15.1
**Time Bar:** None as a hard condition precedent, but notify **immediately upon awareness**. Failure to give an Early Warning affects assessment of a later Compensation Event under clause 61.5.
**Given by:** Contractor or Project Manager.

**Structure:**
- Header: "Early Warning Notice under Clause 15.1 of the NEC4 Engineering and Construction Contract."
- Contract reference and Works Information reference.
- State the matter which could:
  - increase the total of the Prices, or
  - delay Completion, or
  - delay a Key Date, or
  - impair the performance of the works in use.
- Describe the matter factually — date noticed, cause (if known), evidence.
- Propose that the matter be added to the register of Early Warning matters (clause 15.2) and request an Early Warning meeting under clause 15.3 if the Project Manager considers one required.
- Do NOT quantify time or cost impact in the EWN — that belongs in a Compensation Event notification.

## 2. NEC4 ECC — Compensation Event Notification (Clause 61)

**Reference:** Clause 61.3
**Time Bar:** **8 weeks from when the Contractor became aware** of the event. This is a strict condition precedent — miss it and entitlement to both time and money is lost, unless the event is one the Project Manager should have notified themselves under clause 61.1.
**Given by:** Contractor.

**Structure:**
- Header: "Notification of a Compensation Event under Clause 61.3 of the NEC4 Engineering and Construction Contract."
- Contract reference.
- Identify the Compensation Event: quote the specific sub-clause of clause 60.1 relied upon (e.g. "60.1(12) — physical conditions which an experienced contractor would have judged to have such a small chance of occurring that it would have been unreasonable for the Contractor to have allowed for them").
- State the date on which the Contractor became aware of the event.
- Describe the event factually.
- State that a quotation will be submitted within 3 weeks of an instruction to quote (clause 62.3), or that a quotation is enclosed if the Contractor has already assessed it.
- Request the Project Manager notify their decision under clause 61.4 within 1 week.

## 3. NEC4 ECC — Compensation Event Quotation (Clause 62)

**Reference:** Clause 62
**Time Bar:** 3 weeks from Project Manager's instruction to quote.

**Structure:**
- Quotation for the Compensation Event notified on [date].
- Assessment of the change in Defined Cost under clause 63.
- Fee applied at the tendered percentages.
- Change to Completion Date and any Key Date.
- Detailed programme showing the effect on the Accepted Programme.
- Assumptions on which the quotation is based (clause 61.6).
- Alternative quotations if requested by the Project Manager.

## 4. JCT SBC/Q — Notice of Delay (Clause 2.27)

**Reference:** Clause 2.27 (Notice by Contractor of delay to progress) — SBC/Q 2024. Equivalent clause under DB 2024 is 2.24.
**Time Bar:** "Forthwith" once it becomes reasonably apparent that progress is being or is likely to be delayed. Not an absolute bar, but delay in giving notice weakens the Extension of Time position.
**Given by:** Contractor.

**Structure:**
- Header: "Notice of Delay under Clause 2.27 of the JCT Standard Building Contract 2024 [/ Clause 2.24 of JCT Design and Build 2024]."
- Contract reference.
- Identify the material circumstance causing the delay.
- State whether the Contractor considers the cause to be a Relevant Event (quote the specific sub-clause of 2.29 SBC / 2.26 DB — e.g. "2.29.6 — Employer's instructions").
- Give particulars of the expected effects (clause 2.27.2), including the expected delay in the completion of the Works or any Section beyond the relevant Completion Date.
- Note that further particulars will follow as reasonably necessary.
- Do NOT admit any effect on other Contractor obligations or waive any right.

## 5. JCT SBC/Q — Loss and Expense Application (Clause 4.20)

**Reference:** Clause 4.20 (Matters affecting regular progress) — SBC/Q 2024. Equivalent under DB is 4.19.
**Time Bar:** No absolute bar, but the Contractor must "make an application to the Architect/Contract Administrator as soon as it becomes, or should reasonably have become, apparent to the Contractor that regular progress of the Works has been or is likely to be materially affected" (clause 4.20.1).

**Structure:**
- Header: "Application for Loss and/or Expense under Clause 4.20 of the JCT Standard Building Contract 2024."
- Contract reference.
- Identify the Relevant Matter (clause 4.22 SBC / 4.21 DB) — quote the specific sub-clause.
- State the initial assessment of loss and/or expense being incurred or likely to be incurred.
- Provide such information as should reasonably enable the CA/QS to ascertain the loss and expense (clause 4.20.2).
- Reserve the right to submit further information as the loss crystallises.

## 6. Payment mechanism notices (HGCRA / JCT / NEC4)

Under the Housing Grants, Construction and Regeneration Act 1996 (as amended) the statutory payment mechanism applies to all "construction contracts" — bespoke amendments cannot dilute the s.110/111 requirements.

- **Payment Notice** (payer or payee): due within 5 days of the due date. States the sum considered due and the basis of calculation.
- **Pay-Less Notice** (payer): served no later than the prescribed period before the final date for payment (JCT: 5 days; NEC4 typically 7 days — check Y(UK)2). States the reduced sum and the basis.
- **Notice of Intention to Suspend** (payee): 7 days' written notice before suspending performance for non-payment under s.112.

Do not draft a claim response as a Pay-Less Notice unless it is served within the statutory window — a late Pay-Less is invalid and the Notified Sum becomes payable in full.
