# Preliminary Market Engagement Brief Template — UK

Use this template when generating a Preliminary Market Engagement Brief for a PMEN-stage opportunity scoring ≥70. Under the UK Procurement Act 2023, contracting authorities are actively encouraged (and in some cases required by their internal procurement policies) to run pre-market engagement before publishing a Tender Notice. PMENs typically signal 3–12 months of advance notice — this is the relationship-building window.

### Recommended Pre-Market Engagement Actions

The following actions are recommended during the engagement window before the Tender Notice:

1. **Attend the market engagement event** — The PMEN will normally include a briefing day, one-to-one supplier meetings, or a written questionnaire. Attend or respond to all of these; participation is visible to the authority and is often referenced in Award Reports.
2. **Written response to the market engagement questionnaire** — Under the Procurement Act 2023 authorities can lawfully use PME responses to shape scope, KPIs, and evaluation criteria. This is your chance to influence the specification.
3. **Stakeholder Mapping** — Identify the key decision-makers: Procurement Officer, SRO/Senior Responsible Owner, Technical Advisor, Social Value assessor. Map relationships.
4. **Case Study Preparation** — Prepare a targeted case study from a comparable prior project.
5. **Carbon Reduction Plan review** — For contracts >£5m per year (central government), PPN 06/20 requires a compliant CRP at tender. Refresh yours ahead of the Tender Notice.
6. **Social Value scoring familiarisation** — PPN 06/21 mandates a minimum 10% weighting on Social Value across central government procurement, with themes drawn from the Social Value Model. Review the model's Themes, Policy Outcomes, and Model Award Criteria (MACs).
